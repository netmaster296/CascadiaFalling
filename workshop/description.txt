[h1]Prerequisites: install these first, or the mission will not load[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]BepInEx 5[/b] (the Nuclear Option Mod Manager installs it for you).
[*] [b]NOCustomMaps 1.4.2 or newer[/b] (BatongMaster's custom map loader; the 1.4.0 build stopped working with the 2026-10-30 game update, so get 1.4.2).
[*] [b]The Cascadia map, build cm.cascadia.d7766d89[/b]: in the Nuclear Option Mod Manager search for "Cascadia Falling" and install it (it pulls NOCustomMaps in). Without the manager, download cascadia-0.1.1.zip from [url=https://github.com/netmaster296/CascadiaFalling/releases]github.com/netmaster296/CascadiaFalling/releases[/url] and put cascadia-0.1.1.nomapz into BepInEx\plugins\NOCustomMaps\maps\ in your game folder. About 1.6 GB.
[*] [b]Multiplayer:[/b] the host and every player need the same map build. If a map update changes the build key, this page says so and everyone updates the map together.
[/list]

[b]Hosts using Wing Command:[/b] set AiSharpTurns = false in BepInEx\config\com.marci.wingcommand.cfg before you host. With it on, most AI take-offs end in a crash (in our tests 58 of 183 AI take-offs crashed within a minute with it on, 0 of 67 with it off).

[h1]Optional and recommended mods[/h1]
[list]
[*] [b]NOAIFlightFix[/b] (recommended, host only): keeps the AI's climb-outs after take-off and its dives toward low targets in check. [url=https://github.com/netmaster296/NOAIFlightFix/releases]github.com/netmaster296/NOAIFlightFix/releases[/url]
[*] [b]NOLakeIndex[/b] (recommended, everyone): a fast lake and river lookup for NOCustomMaps, for a better frame rate on this map. [url=https://github.com/netmaster296/NOLakeIndex/releases]github.com/netmaster296/NOLakeIndex/releases[/url]
[*] [b]NOAirbaseLightsFix[/b] (optional): keeps the donor airbase's runway lights off on custom maps. [url=https://github.com/netmaster296/NOAirbaseLightsFix/releases]github.com/netmaster296/NOAirbaseLightsFix/releases[/url]
[*] [b]NOCloudDeck[/b] (optional, visual and local only): a Pacific Northwest rain day, with a cloud deck, rain and haze. [b]PvP warning:[/b] players running it see much less far than players without it (in this weather about 5-12 km under the cloud and 0.5 km inside it, against about 50 km in stock), so agree on it before a PvP match. [url=https://github.com/netmaster296/NOCloudDeck/releases]github.com/netmaster296/NOCloudDeck/releases[/url]
[*] [b]NOGridLetters[/b] (optional): grid rows past Z read A*, B*... and zoomed-in columns read 12-3; without it the game shows punctuation and lower-case letters. [url=https://github.com/netmaster296/NOGridLetters/releases]github.com/netmaster296/NOGridLetters/releases[/url]
[/list]
Each is a DLL for BepInEx\plugins\, or install them with the Nuclear Option Mod Manager where they are listed.

[h1]Cascadia Falling[/h1]
PALA attacks Puget Sound from the Pacific and the straits of Juan de Fuca and Georgia; BDF defends from Tacoma. A 7.5-hour war (T+ is real minutes; the clock runs at 10x), and the first fleets arrive after about two hours. PvP: both sides can be joined.

A 400 km map of the Pacific Northwest, Vancouver to Yakima, built from real elevation, land cover and OpenStreetMap data: sixteen airfields laid out as they really are (runways, taxiways and stands at their real coordinates), seventeen FOBs, seven helipads, three naval bases, eight mountain radar sites and four nuclear silo fields.

[b]BDF[/b] starts with nearly everything: four Annex assault carriers and five Argus frigates at its naval bases, Shard corvettes and patrol boats on picket across the Sound, twelve fighters taking off from its own fields at the start, and three relief waves entering from the map border.

[b]PALA[/b] holds CFB Comox, and its fleets start far out at sea. The western carrier group reaches Port Townsend about T+138 and Commencement Bay about T+297, the northern one Elliott Bay about T+353, and the I-5 column reaches the JBLM gate about T+288. A carrier group is a Hyperion, a Dynamo destroyer and three Shard corvettes; a surface group a Dynamo and two Shards; each brings patrol boats and landing craft. Until the fleets arrive the war is air raids, radar strikes, heli-lifts and landings. Every aircraft takes off from a field or carrier its side owns; only PALA strike packages, heli-lifts and BDF relief enter at the map border, low and with full tanks. About 35 AI aircraft fly at once. Everything PALA captures builds for PALA.

[b]As the war unfolds the mission broadcasts intelligence reports[/b] - long-range radar plots, picket contact reports, coast watch and ground liaison - naming what each side's sensors are picking up and where it is headed.

[b]Objectives:[/b] PALA wins by taking JBLM, Fort Lewis FOB, the Port of Tacoma and the Narrows; every other BDF airfield, FOB, pad and naval base is a PALA objective on the way, and each silo field is one to put out of action. BDF wins by sinking all five PALA Hyperions and then destroying the PALA Strategic HQ, which appears on Texada Island once the last carrier goes down; or by holding the four key sites until T+450, which ends the war. Nuclear weapons are cleared at an escalation score of 900 and strategic launchers at 3000; each side keeps a warhead reserve the AI never uses.

[b]Grid callouts:[/b] 10 km cells, A0 at the north-west corner. With NOGridLetters a callout is row + minor row + column, e.g. "D*f12-3".
